# Universal Token Liquidity & Acquisition

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Frame 12 (1) (1).svg" alt=""><figcaption><p>Universal Token Ownership Flow</p></figcaption></figure>

**Acquiring Universal Tokens:**

* Prospective token holders can **purchase Universal tokens** directly from **available markets.**
* When leveraging the Universal relayer, liquidity is **maintained by merchants**, ensuring efficient price discovery and execution.

#### **Redeeming & Burning Universal Tokens:**

* Token holders have the **option to sell their tokens back to merchants**.
* Upon repurchase, **merchants burn the tokens**, reducing the circulating supply and maintaining the integrity of the Universal ecosystem.
