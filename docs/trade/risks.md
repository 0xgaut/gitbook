# Risks & Disclaimers

Please read and understand the risks associated with trading Universal assets before participating.

## General Risks

### Smart Contract Risk

While Universal smart contracts have been audited ([EVM Audit](https://github.com/r0bert-ethack/audits/blob/main/Alongside%20-%20Universal%20Contracts%20report%20-%20Final.pdf), [Solana Audit](https://hacken.io/audits/universal/)), smart contracts may contain undiscovered vulnerabilities.

### Market Risk

- Cryptocurrency prices are volatile and can change rapidly
- Past performance does not guarantee future results
- You may lose some or all of your investment

### Liquidity Risk

- While Universal provides deep liquidity, extreme market conditions may affect execution
- Large orders may experience price impact

### Custodial Risk

Universal assets are backed by reserves held in custody (Coinbase Prime). While custody is with a regulated provider, custodial arrangements carry inherent risks.

## Trading-Specific Risks

### Slippage

Price may move between quote and execution. Set appropriate slippage tolerance to protect against unfavorable price movements.

### Gas Fees

Blockchain gas fees fluctuate based on network congestion. High gas fees may make small trades uneconomical.

### Failed Transactions

Transactions may fail due to:

- Insufficient gas
- Price movement exceeding slippage
- Network congestion
- Expired orders

### Impermanent Loss

If you provide liquidity to uAsset pools, you may experience impermanent loss.

## Regulatory Risks

Cryptocurrency regulations vary by jurisdiction and may change. Ensure you comply with local laws before trading.

### Geographic Restrictions

Universal services may not be available in all jurisdictions. Check [Terms of Service](https://app.universal.xyz/term-of-service) for restrictions.

## Operational Risks

### Merchant Risk

Universal relies on permissioned merchants for minting and redemption. Merchant failures or delays may impact service availability.

### Oracle Risk

Price feeds depend on reliable oracle data. Oracle failures or manipulation could affect pricing.

### Bridge Risk

If using bridged assets alongside uAssets, bridge vulnerabilities may pose additional risks.

## Security Best Practices

### Wallet Security

- Never share your private keys or seed phrase
- Use hardware wallets for large amounts
- Verify all transaction details before signing
- Be cautious of phishing attempts
