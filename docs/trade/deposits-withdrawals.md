# Deposits & Withdrawals

Fund your Universal wallet with native assets from any blockchain, or withdraw your uAssets back to their native chains.

## Overview

Universal lets you bring assets from their native chains into your trading wallet:

- **Deposit**: Send native assets (like XRP, BTC, SOL) → receive uAssets on your connected wallet
- **Withdraw**: Send uAssets → receive native assets on your specified address

This means you can trade assets from any blockchain using a single wallet on your preferred chain.

---

## Deposits

### How It Works

1. You send a native asset (e.g., XRP from the XRP Ledger)
2. Universal verifies the deposit
3. You receive the equivalent uAsset (e.g., uXRP) on your connected wallet
4. Use your uAssets for trading on Universal

### Step-by-Step: Making a Deposit

#### 1. Connect Your Wallet

Visit [app.universalassets.xyz](https://app.universalassets.xyz) and connect the wallet where you want to receive uAssets (e.g., a Base wallet).

#### 2. Complete KYC Verification

Before your first deposit, you'll need to verify your identity:

1. Click **Deposit** in the app
2. Follow the KYC prompts to verify your identity
3. This authorizes your wallet for deposits and withdrawals

**Why KYC?** Regulatory requirements for handling native assets require identity verification. This is a one-time process per wallet.

#### 3. Get Your Deposit Address

After verification:

1. Select the asset you want to deposit (e.g., XRP)
2. Universal generates a unique deposit address for that asset
3. Copy the address carefully

**Important:** Each asset has its own deposit address. Always use the correct address for the asset you're sending.

#### 4. Send Your Assets

From your native wallet (e.g., XRP Ledger wallet):

1. Initiate a transfer to the deposit address
2. Double-check the address before confirming
3. Send the transaction

#### 5. Wait for Confirmation

| Asset Type | Typical Confirmation Time |
|------------|--------------------------|
| Most popular assets | Seconds to minutes |
| Some assets | Up to 2 days |

You'll see your uAssets in your connected wallet once the deposit confirms.

### Deposit Tips

**Start with a small test deposit**
Verify everything works before depositing larger amounts.

**Use the correct network**
Make sure you're sending from the right chain. Sending to the wrong network may result in lost funds.

**Save your deposit address**
You can reuse the same deposit address for future deposits of the same asset.

**Check confirmation requirements**
Some blockchains require more confirmations than others, which affects deposit speed.

## Withdrawals

### How It Works

1. You specify the native chain address where you want to receive funds
2. Your uAssets are burned
3. Native assets are released to your specified address

### Step-by-Step: Making a Withdrawal

#### 1. Initiate Withdrawal

In the Universal app:

1. Click **Withdraw**
2. Select the uAsset you want to withdraw (e.g., uXRP)
3. Enter the amount

#### 2. Enter Your Destination Address

Provide the address on the native chain where you want to receive your assets:

- For XRP: Your XRP Ledger address
- For BTC: Your Bitcoin wallet address
- For SOL: Your Solana wallet address

**Double-check your address.** Withdrawals to incorrect addresses cannot be reversed.

#### 3. Confirm and Sign

1. Review the withdrawal details
2. Sign the transaction in your wallet
3. Your uAssets are burned and the withdrawal processes

#### 4. Receive Your Assets

Native assets are sent to your specified address. Timing depends on the destination chain's confirmation requirements.

### Withdrawal Tips

**Verify address ownership**
Only withdraw to addresses you control. Sending to exchange deposit addresses may require additional steps.

**Account for network fees**
Native chain transaction fees are deducted from your withdrawal amount.

**Keep records**
Save transaction hashes for your records and potential support needs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do I need to KYC?

Handling native assets across chains requires compliance with financial regulations. KYC verification ensures Universal can legally process your deposits and withdrawals.

### Can I deposit from an exchange?

Yes, but make sure to use the correct deposit address for the specific asset. Some exchanges may require you to whitelist the address first.

### What if my deposit doesn't arrive?

1. Check the transaction on the source chain's block explorer
2. Verify you used the correct deposit address
3. Wait for the required number of confirmations
4. Contact support if the issue persists after confirmations complete

### What's the minimum deposit/withdrawal?

Minimums vary by asset. Check the app for specific limits.

### Can I cancel a withdrawal?

Once a withdrawal is signed and submitted, it cannot be cancelled. Native assets will be sent to the specified address.

## Deposits vs. Trading

| Action | Use Case |
|--------|----------|
| **Deposit** | Bring native assets into Universal to get uAssets |
| **Trade** | Swap between uAssets or buy uAssets with USDC |
| **Withdraw** | Convert uAssets back to native assets on their home chain |

Most users trade directly with USDC or existing tokens. Deposits and withdrawals are for users who want to move assets between native chains and Universal.
